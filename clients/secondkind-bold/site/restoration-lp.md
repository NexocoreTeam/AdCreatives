## Section 3: Under-supplied (the mechanism)

**Header:** She's not gone. She's under-supplied.

**Body:**

Your gut bacteria produce compounds called postbiotics. They're what actually run the day-to-day: digestion, the upkeep of your gut lining, steady energy, and the constant signaling between your gut and your brain. Around 90% of your serotonin is made in your gut, not your head.

When production drops, and it drops with stress, age, antibiotics, and hormonal shifts, everything dims a little. Digestion gets unpredictable. Energy gets negotiable. Calm gets rare.

Not broken. Under-supplied.

**Sub-block: why the usual fix didn't work**

The usual fix is a probiotic: swallow live bacteria and hope they arrive, survive, and eventually start producing. About 70% die in transit. Most of the rest never colonize. If you've finished a bottle and felt nothing, that's why. It was never you.

**Pivot:**

Gut Balance skips the survival problem entirely. It delivers the compounds themselves: three patented postbiotics, already made, already active on arrival. No live bacteria. Nothing that has to win a fight in your stomach first.

## Section 8: FAQ

**Why three months?**
Because that's what the science measured, not a number we invented. Regularity outcomes were studied at 30 days. GI symptoms, perceived stress, and quality of life were measured at 84 days. Give the system its window, and your first 60 days are covered by the guarantee either way.

**How is this different from the probiotics I've tried?**
It's a different category, not a better version of the same thing. Probiotics send live bacteria and hope they survive the trip and colonize; about 70% don't survive at all. Postbiotics are the compounds those bacteria were supposed to produce, delivered directly, already active. Nothing has to survive anything.

**Why does it cost more than other gut supplements?**
We could be cheaper. The way to do that is the way everyone else does it: live bacteria, a big number on the label, and most of it dead before it reaches your gut. You've probably bought that product before, so you already know what it really costs. What you're paying for here is the part the cheap version skips: three patented compounds, each with its own human trial, third-party tested. The cheap option is only cheap if it works.

**What if it doesn't work for me?**
Then you don't pay. Try your first bottle for up to 60 days from delivery. If you don't feel a meaningful shift, email us with your order number and you'll get a full refund of the purchase price. No return shipment required in most cases.

**What exactly is in it?**
Three patented postbiotics: Totipro®, EpiCor®, and Bereum®, together as the BiomeBalance™ complex. Vegan, non-GMO, gluten-free, kosher, third-party tested, made in the USA. No live bacteria, so no refrigeration. Two capsules a day.

**When will I feel something?**
Honestly: many people notice digestion settling in the first weeks, which is consistent with the 30-day regularity study. The stress and quality-of-life outcomes were measured at 84 days. We tell you the real timeline upfront, because the brands that promised overnight results never cited anything.
