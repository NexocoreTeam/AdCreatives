# SecondKind Bold — Phase 1 Strategic Synthesis

*Generated 2026-05-23. Reference doc for the bold-variant paid acquisition launch. Pulls from `brand.yaml`, `positioning.md`, all 6 personas, the Tier-3 social scrape (282 comments across IG/TT/YT), VOC corpus (`voc/extracted_pains.yaml`), gap map (`research/competitive-gaps.yaml`), and creative matrix (`strategy/creative_matrix.yaml`, 52 rows across 4 tabs).*

---

## Executive summary

The probiotic category has spent two decades selling dead bacteria. Five of the six SecondKind ICPs already suspect this in their bodies — they just don't have language for it. The bold variant's only job is to give them that language, prove the indictment with receipts in the next line, and convert them on mechanism. The single biggest lever is **vindication before conversion** — naming what they already suspected and exonerating them for it ("you weren't wrong, the product was") before introducing postbiotics as the missing piece. That arc fits the dominant heuristic pair (`framing_effect` + `authority_bias`) across all 6 personas. The matrix has 52 rows; the top 10 are ranked at the bottom of this doc, and the one to ship first is `pain-010`.

## 1. The bold thesis

Captured in three lines from `positioning.md`:

> **We don't sell bacteria. We sell what your gut was waiting for them to produce.**

> The probiotic-industrial complex is the named enemy at the **category level**. The customer is the protagonist; the voice always sides with her against the industry. **Wry, not sneering.** Every claim is backed by a receipt in the next line.

> The four-beat arc on every piece: **name the suspicion she already had → diagnose the mechanism → vindicate her → convert with the offer.**

### Receipts in the arsenal (load-bearing facts)
| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| ~70% of live probiotic bacteria die in gastric transit | Widely documented in nutritional science literature |
| EpiCor® — 17% reduction in cold/flu symptom days | 12-week RCT |
| Bereum® — improved GI symptoms, perceived stress, quality of life | 84-day human study |
| Totipro® — bowel regularity outcomes | 30-day clinical trial |
| The colonization problem (live bacteria don't reliably establish) | Standing nutritional-science consensus |
| The plateau effect (initial relief regresses within months) | Standing nutritional-science consensus + repeated VOC pattern |

### Hard rules (from `brand.yaml`)
- **Never** name competitors by brand. Category-level indictment only.
- **Never** mock the customer's past purchases. The cut lands on the industry or the mechanism, never on her.
- **Never** bash doctors. Frame as "literature ahead of practice."
- **Never** lead with operational claims (60-day guarantee, refund). Those close. Lead with mechanism.
- Body / aesthetic shame is permitted **only when the punch lands on the mechanism, not on her** — and is off-limits for Natalie (postpartum) entirely.

### Cross-persona observations

1. **`authority_bias` and `framing_effect` are dominant heuristics on all 6 personas.** Every ad in Phase 1 must satisfy both — the receipt has to be specific *and* the frame has to do the conversion work.
2. **`scarcity` and `temporal_discounting` are weak on all 6.** Countdown timers, "feel better today," "limited time" — all categorically off-limits across the entire creative system. The buyer cohort has already been burned by urgency in this category; reusing the gimmick erodes trust.
3. **The emotional quadrant split is the cleanest segmentation lever.** Two of six (Danielle, Paul) sit in LV/HI — active frustration, professional exposure. Four of six (Isaac, Natalie, Paula, Brandon) sit in LV/LI or HV/LI — normalized compromise. **Two creative modes needed:** activate the acute cost for LV/HI; name the quiet compromise before offering exit for LV/LI.
4. **One shared mechanism wedge converts five of six.** Probiotics-as-delivery-vehicle vs. postbiotics-as-active-compound. The conceptual hinge is structural, not preferential — Natalie is the only persona where mechanism is secondary to safety/transparency framing.

## 3. The psychology framework — Sarah Levinger triple-stack

Captured at `prompts/skills/psychology-profiling.md`. Three frameworks layered into a single diagnostic. The personas above were profiled against all three; this is the reference for *why* certain ads will and won't work on this cohort.

### Layer 1 — The 9 Heuristics (purchase decision shortcuts)

A **heuristic** is a mental shortcut the brain uses to decide quickly without analyzing every detail. There are nine well-documented heuristics that drive ad performance. Every ad activates one or more — sometimes deliberately, sometimes accidentally. The matrix tags every row with the single heuristic it leans on hardest. Same row can be re-tested under a different heuristic, but the lead heuristic determines the headline and the lead visual.

At-a-glance table first, then plain-English definitions of each heuristic below.

| Heuristic | One-line gist | Dominant for | Weak for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scarcity` | Limited time / quantity drives action | — | **All 6** (avoid universally) |
| `social_proof` | "Others like me already do this" | Natalie, Paul, Brandon (peer-mediated) | Brandon distrusts mass / Paul demands credentialed |
| `authority_bias` | Trust expert / credential / data | **All 6** (universal) | None |
| `effect_heuristic` | Gut feel / aesthetics / "feels right" | Danielle, Natalie, Paula (secondary) | Brandon, Paul, Isaac (analytical) |
| `processing_fluency` | Easy-to-understand = trustworthy | Natalie | Brandon, Paul (want depth) |
| `temporal_discounting` | Immediate over long-term | — | **All 6** (avoid universally) |
| `salience_bias` | Attention grabbed by what stands out | Universal (execution-layer) | — |
| `goal_gradient` | Accelerate as finish line nears | — | None dominant; cohort isn't on a program |
| `framing_effect` | How info is presented changes decision | **All 6** (universal) | None |

#### Plain-English definitions

1. **`scarcity`** — Fear of missing out. Countdown timers, "only 12 left." **Skip** — our cohort reads manufactured urgency as a tell.
2. **`social_proof`** — Trust what others like you are doing. Testimonials, UGC, creator endorsements. Use the *peer-mediated* version only (friend, credentialed practitioner) — never generic "everyone's using it."
3. **`authority_bias`** — Trust what credentialed experts say. Doctors, named studies, RCTs. **Dominant for all 6 personas.** Every claim needs a receipt in the next line.
4. **`effect_heuristic`** — "If it feels good, it must be good." Aesthetics and mood do the persuading. Works for Danielle / Natalie / Paula; weak for the analytical half. Always pair with framing or authority.
5. **`processing_fluency`** — Easy-to-understand reads as trustworthy. Clean layouts, short headlines, one claim per ad. The bold variant's voice rule ("short declarative + receipt") satisfies this naturally.
6. **`temporal_discounting`** — Now beats later. "Instant relief," "by tomorrow." **Skip** — the cohort's been burned by quick-results hype already.
7. **`salience_bias`** — Whatever stands out in the scroll wins the click. Bold typography on black, unexpected visuals, stark whitespace. Always-on — our editorial-minimalist aesthetic *is* a salience play.
8. **`goal_gradient`** — Motivation rises as you near the finish line. Progress bars, loyalty ladders. **Skip** — the cohort is stack-fatigued, not on a program.
9. **`framing_effect`** — How you say it changes the decision, even when the facts don't change. "$30/month" vs "$1/day." **Load-bearing for the bold variant** — every ad reframes what the customer already believed.

**Implication:** The dominant heuristic pair across the cohort is **`authority_bias` + `framing_effect`** — every Phase 1 ad must do both. Present a specific receipt (authority) AND reframe what the customer already believed (framing). The bold variant's four-beat arc — *name the suspicion → diagnose the mechanism → vindicate → convert* — is structurally framing+authority all the way down. Ads that lean on aesthetics-only (`effect_heuristic`) work for Danielle/Natalie/Paula but underperform on the analytical half of the cohort.

### Layer 2 — Valence × Intensity quadrants (emotional positioning)

Two axes determine the emotional shape of an ad:

- **Valence** — does the ad pull from *gain* (positive) or *loss / fear / frustration* (negative)? Not "happy vs sad" — which direction the motivation pulls.
- **Intensity** — how *hot* is the emotion? *High* = acute, in-the-moment ("I have to fix this now"). *Low* = quiet, accumulated, normalized ("this used to mean more than this").

Crossed together = 4 quadrants. Abbreviated **HV/HI**, **HV/LI**, **LV/HI**, **LV/LI** (H/L = High/Low, V = Valence, I = Intensity).

| Quadrant | Feeling | What the ad sounds like | Personas (primary) | Lead frame |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HV / HI** — High-positive, high-heat | Breakthrough, transformation | "This will make me unstoppable" | (none primary) | "Stop swallowing corpses" — only at solution-aware retargeting |
| **HV / LI** — High-positive, low-heat | Relief, permission, recognition | "I can stop fighting this" | Natalie | "You can stop fighting this" |
| **LV / HI** — Low/negative, high-heat | Fear of mistake, "stop the bleeding" | "I have to fix this now" | Danielle, Paul | "You've been paying for the wrong category" |
| **LV / LI** — Low/negative, low-heat | Dull dissatisfaction, accepted compromise | "This used to mean more than this" | Isaac, Paula, Brandon | "You stopped expecting more. Maybe restart that." |

**Implication:** **Most of the cohort sits in LV territory** (5 of 6) — meaning the motivating emotion is loss-flavored, not gain-flavored. Two of them sit at high heat (Danielle is acutely frustrated; Paul is acutely professionally exposed); three sit at low heat (Isaac, Paula, Brandon have all *normalized* the disappointment over months or years). The bold variant's natural register is LV/HI — indict the category, expose the mechanism cost. For the LV/LI majority, the variant must downshift intensity — name the quiet compromise first, indict more softly, let the data do the prosecuting. **Never** lead with HV/HI breakthrough hype ("transform your life," "the supplement that changes everything") — these personas were burned by exactly that hype already and the pattern-match will tank the ad.

### Layer 3 — The 14 Paired-Mechanism Templates (Tether Lab Prompt Library)

A **paired mechanism** is a two-part ad recipe: one part hooks attention, the other closes the sale. There are 14 templates in the Levinger library. For this cohort, **four show up across 3+ personas** — those are our core ad shapes.

1. **`first_principles_plus_loss_aversion`** — Explain how the thing actually works, then name what they lose by sticking with the old option.
 *Ex: "Probiotics are supposed to reach the gut and produce bioactive compounds. ~70% die before they get there. You've been paying for the delivery vehicle, not the payload."* For: Danielle, Paul, Paula, Brandon. **Bold variant's primary template** — most top-10 matrix rows use it.

2. **`reframing_perception_plus_emotional_trigger`** — Flip how the customer sees the problem, then connect it to a felt moment.
 *Ex: "That's not you. That's the die-off." over a 7pm bloat shot.* For: Isaac, Natalie. The "you can stop fighting this" pairing.

3. **`authority_borrowing_plus_data_insight`** — Credentialed third-party voice + specific named statistic.
 *Ex: Dr. Shin (microbiologist) quote, then "Bereum® — 84-day human study, GI symptoms improved."* For: Isaac, Paul, Brandon. Analytical buyers trust a named outsider over the brand voice.

4. **`counterintuitive_insight_plus_specificity`** — Fact that contradicts the customer's assumption + immediate named specific.
 *Ex: "Perimenopause depletes postbiotic output independent of estrogen. EpiCor® — 12-week RCT — 17% reduction in symptom days."* For: Paula, Brandon. The "literature is ahead of practice" pairing.

**Pairings to avoid for this cohort:**
- `gamification_plus_time_sensitive_offer` — progress bars + countdown timers (urgency is allergic).
- `shock_factor_plus_transformation_shortcut` — "weird trick" + "transform overnight" (hype is allergic).
- `curiosity_plus_reverse_psychology` — open-loop teasers / "don't buy this" framing (cleverness reads gimmicky).

There are two layered models to understand where any single ad lands.

### Axis B — Mental Stage (per ad / per moment)
*Added in commit 926e062 — the "where their head is when the ad lands" axis.*

| Stage | What's happening | Best brief shape |
|---|---|---|
| **trigger** | A specific moment (bloated dinner, missed deadline, third illness) just shoved them into active seeking | Lean into the trigger moment verbatim |
| **exploration** | Researching, comparing, reading labels | First-principles mechanism explainers |
| **evaluation** | Already convinced of category, picking a brand | Receipts: named ingredients, named trials, named outcomes |
| **purchase** | Card out, "convince me to click" | Permission + decision confidence (not urgency) |

**Implication:** *Even within a single problem_aware persona, briefs should span all four mental stages.* The customer cycles. The same Done-Everything Danielle is in "trigger" mode at 7pm post-bloat and in "evaluation" mode at 10am scrolling LinkedIn. **A Phase 1 brief batch should cover all four mental stages per priority persona,** not just the persona's awareness level.

## 9. The biggest levers (synthesis)

If we strip everything above down to the levers most likely to produce conversions in Phase 1, four show up:

### Lever 1 — Vindication before conversion
*The single biggest lever.* Every persona except Natalie sits at the intersection of frustration + identity-threat about being "the kind of person who falls for marketing." The bold variant exonerates them before selling — "you weren't wrong, the product was" — which converts both the analytical personas (it satisfies their `framing_effect`) and the affective personas (it satisfies their `effect_heuristic` via felt validation). This lever lives in `pain-010`, `hook-002`, `love-010`, and the LV/HI version of any retargeting ad.

### Lever 2 — Mechanism reframe with a receipt in the next line
The category's failure mode is hype-without-receipts. The bold variant's voice rule is "every claim is backed by a receipt in the next line." This is **the strongest differentiator from the competitive set** because it's structural, not stylistic. Seed, Arrae, and Ritual can't adopt this without abandoning their existing claims. Lives in `pain-002`, `pain-006`, `pain-008`, and `hook-001`.

### Lever 3 — Body indictment that lands on the mechanism, not the body
Bloating is the cohort's #1 pain. The bold variant has unique permission to attack it because the mechanism reframe redirects causality from her body to the product ("that's not you, that's the die-off"). The measured variant can't do this — it would read as body-shaming. The bold variant can — because the cut lands on the mechanism. **Off-limits for Natalie.** Lives in `pain-012`.

### Lever 4 — Practitioner-channel expansion (Paul)
Paul is the only solution-aware persona, which means he's the only one ready for receipts-first ads instead of vindication-first. He's also the highest-trust-bridge persona — a recommendation from him reaches 50-500 patients. Phase 1 should include at least one practitioner-to-practitioner long-form (LinkedIn carousel, IG mechanism reel, or trade-podcast guest spot) targeting him. ROI math: even one Paul conversion = 50-500 downstream funnel entries. Not in the top 10 (because he's a different brief shape), but **strategically the cheapest scale lever.**

## 10. What to be careful about

### Voice discipline (Sarah Levinger framework constraints)
- **Never** use `scarcity` or `temporal_discounting` mechanics. The cohort is allergic. Countdown timers, "feel better today," limited-time framing — all read as the manufactured-urgency move that drove them away from probiotics.
- **Never** lead with `shock_factor_plus_transformation_shortcut`. "Miracle," "revolutionary," "life-changing" — all on the prohibited-terms list for a reason.
- **Never** use `effect_heuristic` alone. Aesthetics-only ads work for Danielle/Natalie/Paula but fail for the analytical half of the cohort. Pair effect with framing or authority.

### VOC bias awareness
The IG comment scrape skews to brand followers. Detractors and churned customers are underrepresented. **Lean on the competitive-gaps cancellation quotes as primary evidence** (those are real abandonment moments), and treat the social-comment language as secondary color.

### Frame discipline by persona
- **Natalie** — body shame is off-limits. The frame is *return* to a prior self, not transformation.
- **Brandon** — replacement framing kills the sale ("you bought wrong"). Completion framing earns it ("you were missing the active compound").
- **Paula** — the structural objection is "if it's hormonal, gut products can't fix it." Mechanism reframe is mandatory before any product mention.
- **Paul** — practitioner-to-practitioner only. Never lifestyle imagery, never customer-voice testimonials. Trade-publication aesthetic.
- **Isaac** — male peer voice, not influencer. Podcast-clip register. No smiling stock people.
