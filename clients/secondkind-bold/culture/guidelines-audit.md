# Guidelines Audit — What's Law, What's Identity, What's Just Taste

**Date:** 2026-06-10 · **Trigger:** operator direction that the guidelines may be over-restricting copy and that the system must improve over time instead of defending its own documents.

---

## The diagnosis

The current guidelines conflate four different kinds of rules and present them with equal weight. When a legal rule, a soul rule, and a stylistic preference all read as equally untouchable, every writer (human or Claude) defaults to maximum restriction, and the copy converges toward the safest interpretation of every rule simultaneously. That is exactly how "bold" copy comes out sounding house-trained.

The fix is not deleting the guidelines. It is sorting every rule into a layer, so we know which lines are walls and which are chalk.

**Supporting fact from the research:** FDA/FTC enforcement in this category targets disease claims, missing substantiation, and undisclosed influencer relationships. It does not police tone, humor, darkness, or weirdness. The March 2025 joint warning letters were about diabetes treatment claims. Nothing in federal law prevents us from being funny, gross, specific, or strange. The legal layer is narrow and precise; everything else is choice.

---

## The four layers

### Layer 0 — Law (never breaks, no exceptions)
- No disease claims: cure, treats, heals, prevents, reverses, eliminates. (FDA/FTC, RCT-level substantiation expected for health claims.)
- Every efficacy claim must be substantiated somewhere reachable (we have the trials; this is an asset, not a constraint).
- Influencer/UGC content is fully in regulatory scope: disclosure rules apply.
- Structure-function qualifiers live in fine print on paid + LP surfaces.

### Layer 1 — Identity (the soul; breaking these isn't bold, it's brand suicide)
- We side with her against the category. The cut lands on the industry, the mechanism, or ourselves. Never on her, her choices, her intelligence, or her body as antagonist.
- Vindication is the arc: she was right, the product was wrong.
- Receipts exist and are one tap away (placement can flex; existence cannot).
- Never mock her past purchases. Never bash doctors (literature ahead of practice).
- Natalie's body is sacred ground. Non-negotiable.
- Never punch down, ever.
- Operator identity: no em-dashes or en-dashes in anything.

### Layer 2 — Taste defaults (good defaults, breakable with evidence, per surface)
This is most of the current rulebook. Every rule here gets a disposition below.

### Layer 3 — Culture (changes weekly; was missing from the system entirely)
Vernacular, memes, sounds, formats, moods. Lives in `vocabulary.yaml` and `pulse-*.md`, with expiry dates. Wins all conflicts about register and word choice on organic surfaces, loses all conflicts with Layers 0-1.

---

## Surface tiers (the second missing concept)

One rulebook governed every surface. Replace with four tiers of license:

| Tier | Surface | License |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Comment replies, community posts | Maximum. Full group-chat register. Questions, 💀, lowercase, banter. Layer 0-1 only. |
| T2 | Organic TikTok/Reels | High. Trend-riding, jokes without citations in-frame (receipt in pinned comment or caption). |
| T3 | Paid, organic-style (Spark-type, love letters) | Medium. Current voice doc largely applies; culture layer feeds vocabulary. |
| T4 | Paid hooks/statics, LPs | Tightest. Full existing rulebook + FDA fine print. |

A line earns its way up tiers with evidence (see bold-bets.md ladder).

## Rule-by-rule disposition

### KEEP (Layer 0 or 1, plus defaults that survived audit)
| Rule | Layer | Note |
|---|---|---|
| FDA prohibited terms | 0 | Law. |
| Claims substantiated | 0 | Law, and our moat: we are the only ones in the conversation holding RCTs. |
| Cut never lands on her | 1 | The brand. |
| Never mock past purchases | 1 | The vindication arc depends on it. |
| Never bash doctors | 1 | Strategic (Paul) and accurate to the audience's actual feeling (disappointment, not hatred). |
| Natalie body rules | 1 | Ethics. |
| No em/en dashes | 1 | Operator identity. |
| Never lead with operational claims (guarantee opens) | 2, keep | Still correct: offer closes, never opens. |
| No manufactured scarcity/urgency on offers | 2, keep | Audience-validated weak heuristic. See FLEX for cultural urgency. |
| No sincere wellness-cheese (journey, unlock, holistic...) | 2, keep | See FLEX for ironic quotation. |
| Don't lead hooks with patented ingredient names (except Brandon/Paul) | 2, keep | Already right. |

### FLEX (breakable by surface tier, with the evidence noted)
| Rule as written | New disposition | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "We don't ask, we tell" (no rhetorical questions) | T4 keeps it. T1-T2: questions are the native engagement mechanic; comment-bait drives the first-60-min density the algorithm rewards. | Comment density is an algorithmic distribution input. The rule was an anti-clickbait rule for paid headlines that accidentally governed every surface. |
| Sentence case always | T3-T4 keep it (operator has rejected all-lowercase prose as "basic," that stands). T1-T2 overlays/replies: platform-native casing may be tested A/B. | Lowercase in TikTok overlays is camouflage, not sloppiness. Test, don't switch; operator reviews results. |
| No ALL-CAPS lines | T4 keeps. T1-T2: a single caps punch line is native overlay rhythm. | Format convention, not identity. |
| Approved emoji palette (bans 💀😭🙏) | Keep on Meta captions. T1-T2 TikTok: comment-native emoji are vocabulary, not decoration. | 💀 means "too real." Banning it on TikTok is banning fluency. |
| Receipt in the next line | T3-T4 keep. T1-T2: receipt must be one tap away (pinned comment, caption), not necessarily in-frame. | A meme with a citation in-frame is no longer a meme. The pinned-comment receipt is itself a format the algorithm rewards. |
| Never name competitors | T3-T4 keep (legal exposure + punching-down optics in paid). T1: when a commenter names them first ("is this better than Seed?"), answer honestly, mechanism-first, never disparaging. T2: react to category props and claims ("the internal shower," "53 billion CFUs"), still not brand names. | Comparative advertising is legal when truthful, but the strategic posture (indict the category, not the brand) is right. The gap was having no answer for when SHE names them. |
| Body/aesthetic framing rules | CRITICAL CLARIFICATION: shame is banned; pride and solidarity are encouraged (Danielle, Paula only). "Hot girls know which bathrooms to trust" is reclamation, not shame. | The cautious reading of the shame ban was blocking the single most validated cultural vehicle in the niche. |
| Wellness-cheese ban | Sincere use stays banned. Ironic quotation as category-mockery is allowed on T1-T2 ("your gut journey," eye roll, as an attack on the slop). | The ban exists to avoid sounding like the category. Mocking the category's language is the opposite of sounding like it. |
| No urgency | Offer urgency stays banned. Cultural urgency is exempt: "this sound dies Friday" is true and isn't about inventory. | Trend-riding requires speed language. |

### RETIRE / REPLACE
| Old assumption | Replacement |
|---|---|
| One rulebook for every surface | The four surface tiers above. |
| Guidelines are complete and static | Guidelines = Layers 0-2. Layer 3 lives in culture/ artifacts with expiry dates. The culture layer wins register conflicts on T1-T2. |
| Pre-flight checklist on everything | T1-T2 get the fast lane: the six-question gate in bold-bets.md (runs in minutes), not the full checklist. Full checklist remains for T3-T4. |
| Voice changes require rewriting the docs | The promotion ladder (bold-bets.md): evidence moves a line up tiers; two months of survival at T3 = a proposed guideline edit. The system updates itself on schedule, not on argument. |

---

## The one-sentence version

Walls: claims discipline and whose side we're on. Chalk: everything about how we sound. The chalk gets redrawn by the culture layer on a schedule, and evidence, not documents, decides what graduates to the paid surfaces.
