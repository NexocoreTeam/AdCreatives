## The six-question gate (run before any bet ships; minutes, not meetings)

1. **Layer 0 clean?** No disease claims, substantiation reachable.
2. **Layer 1 intact?** The cut lands on the category, the mechanism, or us. Never on her.
3. **Live evidence?** At least 2 independent current signals (pulse, vocabulary entry, scraped comments) that this audience talks this way now.
4. **The louder friend test:** would she screenshot it to the group chat (share) or screenshot it to dunk (cringe)? Gut call, but make it explicitly.
5. **Entry rung available?** Can it debut on T1/T2 where blast radius is one comment thread or one organic post?
6. **Kill date + metric named?** Before it ships, not after.

Six yes = green light. No further permission needed. This gate replaces the full pre-flight checklist on T1-T2 surfaces (the fast lane).

## The promotion ladder (how the system improves instead of staying stubborn)

```
T1 comment reply  →  T2 organic post  →  T3 paid organic-style  →  T4 paid hook
     (hours)            (days)              (1-2 weeks test)         (sprint)
```

- A line/register that wins at one rung earns a test at the next. Evidence, not opinion, moves it.
- **Promotion to guidelines:** any bet that survives at T3 for 60 days becomes a proposed edit to the voice docs (Layer 2 default changes). The documents follow the evidence, never the reverse.
- **Kill discipline:** every bet has a kill date. Dead bets get a one-line post-mortem here, then archive. Stubbornness in either direction (defending old rules OR defending pet bets) loses to the ladder.
- **Tagging:** every shipped creative notes `bends:` (which Layer 2 defaults it bent) in its brief/post log, so performance can be correlated with boldness later.
