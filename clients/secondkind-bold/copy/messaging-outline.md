## 1. The core idea (the platform)

- **Restoration, not transformation.** Everyone says "I just want to feel like myself again," so that's the promise: the return of the person you already were, not a rebirth. The words "transformational / life-changing" stay banned; the feeling is delivered through restoration framing.
- **"Meet the second kind of you."** The brand name carries it: the you managing a body vs. the you who never had to.
- **The licensing receipt:** the 84-day human study measured GI symptoms, perceived stress, AND quality of life. That citation is what lets whole-life claims fly.
- **The confidence doctrine:** we say what only we can say. Different, not better. Anchored to what nobody can copy: the output-not-input mechanism, the patented trio, a human trial per ingredient, the 90-day dare with money-back.

## 5. The 90-Day Challenge (angle approved; wording open)

The mechanic: the guarantee is the dare, the 84-day science is the reason.

- The charm receipt: **"The longest trial behind Gut Balance ran 84 days. We round up."**
- The arc: Days 1-30 the settling (regularity studied at 30 days) → Days 31-60 the compounding (inside the guarantee) → Days 61-90 the forgetting (the 84-day marks: GI, stress, quality of life).
- Dare lines: "Give us 90 days. Prove us wrong. We'll pay for the attempt." / "90 days. If we're wrong, it's free. If we're right, you're back." / "The ask is 90 days. Days 1 through 60 are on us." / "Three months. Then judge." / "Give it three months, then try to remember what you were worried about."
- Identity line: "You keep saying 'I'm not usually like this.' Give us 90 days to make that true again."
- Confidence line: "That's not marketing courage. That's what having trials feels like."
- ⚠️ Merchandising rule: guarantee covers first SINGLE bottles only, so the challenge sells as a subscription, never a 3-pack.

## 7. Confidence-doctrine line bank (doctrine approved; lines ready to deploy)

- "We're not a better probiotic. We're not a probiotic at all."
- "Every brand says theirs works. We sell the part that does the working."
- "Claims anyone can make: cleaner, stronger, better. Claims only we can make: it's already made."
- "Nobody else says this because nobody else can: the compounds are already in the capsule."
- Rule for new lines: if any brand could say it, cut it. Keep "only" defensible: anchor it to the patented trio, or carry exclusivity structurally ("We're the ones actually selling them").

## 8. Voice & style rules (extracted from operator feedback)

- Punchy over long. Empowering over clinical-flat. Bolder beats safer.
- Instantly clear: the line must land with zero category knowledge (most people don't know probiotics die in transit).
- Not gender-locked in headers.
- Hedge the science ("about 70%," "studied over 84 days"); a receipt lives within reach of every claim.
- Sentence case; no em-dashes; no all-lowercase prose; guarantee always stated accurately.
- Banned: transformational, life-changing, miracle, journey, "gut reset" (that's the dabbler vocabulary we cut against).

## 9. Approved formats (production specs)

- **TikTok love letter:** 35-75 words, 3 beats: warm callout + hedged fact → emoji-led "supports" lines → product named once + easy close. Scripts: `tiktok-love-letters-v3.md`.
- **POV short:** 1-2 line meme-native identity flex (or POV open) on a candid-motion clip; the messaging test bed. Bank: `tiktok-pov-shorts.md`.
- **The caption structure:** 🤦‍♀️ confession → "turns out" → 💡 vindication turn → mechanism → ✅ postbiotics → offer close.
- **Offer headline pattern:** "$10 off. It was never you." (offer + vindication in the button slot).
