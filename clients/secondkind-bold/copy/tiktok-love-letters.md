## Part 1 — How we talk in this format (the research)

### 1. The culture already said the quiet part. The category didn't.

#guttok sits around 1B views, #hotgirlswithIBS at 110M+, #IBStok at 690M. "Hot girls have stomach issues" made gut dysfunction an identity badge: women discuss it with dark humor, specificity, and zero shame. Meanwhile every brand in the category still says "bloating" and "digestive discomfort" like tasteful abstract nouns. The love letter is the surface where SecondKind Bold matches the comment section's honesty instead of the category's politeness. This is the same territory voice.handwritten.md claims (the group-chat register, the bathroom-and-bedroom logistics). The culture has now validated it at scale.

### 2. Borrow constructions, never vocabulary.

Our buyer is 28-50. Current teen slang (delulu, skibidi, aura) expires in months and reads as a brand in costume. What's durable for this audience is the *construction*, not the word:

- "This is a love letter to..." (the format's native opener, name it literally)
- "To the girls who..." / "To everyone who..."
- The knowing fragment: "By name." / "You know the one." / "Most never do."
- "POV:" only for the short-POV sibling format, not the letter

The actual scroll-stopper is forensic specificity of lived detail: the named bathroom, the red light, the fake early morning. Specificity never ages out. Slang does.

### 3. Write for the reveal, not the paragraph.

Current overlay style: small, simple, centered text, one line per screen. So every line must survive alone on screen for ~2 seconds. The rhythm that works: two or three setup lines of 8-14 words, then a punch fragment of 2-5 words. The fragment is the dopamine. If a line can't be read aloud in one breath, split it.

### 4. The friend opens, the scientist closes.

The register switch from voice.handwritten.md is the engine of this format:

- Beats 1-2 (opener + confessional run): her exact day, in her words. No stats, no brand.
- Beat 3 (the turn): "Here's the part nobody said out loud." The receipt arrives. Roughly 70% transit death, colonization failure, the named trials. Clinical, calm.
- Beat 4 (vindication): "You weren't doing it wrong." It lands because beats 1-2 proved we know her.
- Beat 5 (close): Gut Balance, the mechanism in one line, the 60-day guarantee. Quiet, declarative, zero urgency.

### 5. The one test every line must pass.

Would she say this about herself, to her closest friend? "You unbuttoned your jeans at a red light" passes. "Stop letting bloat ruin your life" fails (we're scolding her). The cut lands on the category or the mechanism. Never on her, her choices, or her body as antagonist.

### 6. Hard rules, unchanged.

Sentence case always, never all-lowercase. No em-dashes or en-dashes anywhere. No competitor names. No scarcity or urgency. No wellness-cheese (journey, transform, unlock, holistic...). FDA structure-function safe: experiential claims plus cited trials, no cure/treat/heal/prevent. Guarantee closes, never opens. Persona body rules: Danielle and Paula full confessional, Isaac performance-coded (never the body), Natalie identity and function only (body is sacred ground), Brandon dry and analytical.
