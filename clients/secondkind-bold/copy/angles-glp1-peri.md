## The strategic read

**GLP-1 is our fight.** Two wedges, both ownable:

1. **The changed-terrain argument.** A GLP-1 gut is a different environment (slowed transit, changed intake). Live-bacteria colonization was already failing in normal guts (~70% transit death); in a changed gut it's an even worse bet. Postbiotics don't colonize, don't need the gut to cooperate. This is our existing burning-building argument aimed at the biggest health conversation in the country.
2. **The reduced-input argument (novel, nobody runs this).** Your gut bacteria ferment what you eat (especially fiber) into the compounds that run digestion, energy, and calm. Eat half as much and the input drops. Postbiotics ARE the output, delivered directly, independent of appetite. Claim-safe as mechanism education + structure-function support.

**Peri scales through the estrobolome.** The gut microbiome's estrogen-modulating machinery (beta-glucuronidase activity recycling estrogen) is real, published, and unknown to consumers. It's the peri 70%-stat: a named mechanism for "nobody told you." We educate on the gut-estrogen axis and keep product claims strictly gut-side (comfort, regularity, stress, clarity via gut-brain).

## The compliance lane for GLP-1 (Layer 0, read first)

- Say "GLP-1" generically. Never name drug brands in ads.
- NEVER claim the product treats, prevents, or reduces drug side effects, or affects how the drug works. That is drug-claim territory.
- The compliant lanes: (a) first-person creator experience ("what I did with my gut," with results-vary disclaimer), (b) mechanism education at category level, (c) structure-function support claims with our trials (digestive comfort, regularity, stress).
- Every script includes a "your doctor decides the drug part" gesture. We are the gut routine, not the drug advice.
- Run GLP-1 creative as creator/Spark-style first (T2/T3); platform policy around GLP-1 content is twitchy, testimonial voice is the safer entry.
