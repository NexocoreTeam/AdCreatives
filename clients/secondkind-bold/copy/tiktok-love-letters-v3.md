## The format spec (extracted from operator-provided examples)

35-75 words. Three beats. 20-40 seconds. Text overlay or VO over simple B-roll / creator content / AI UGC avatar.

1. **Callout + hook fact.** Warm direct address ("girls," "hot girls," "guys," "ladies") plus ONE surprising, hedged fact or identity behavior. Hedge the science: "around," "about," "appears to."
2. **The education.** 2-3 emoji-led, ingredient-level lines in structure-function language ("X supports Y"). Receipts said casually ("studied over 84 days," "in a 12-week study"). This is where compound names live (EpiCor, Bereum, Totipro).
3. **Product + effortless close.** Brand named once, then a low-key close ("two capsules a day," "easy add") and optionally one smart kicker reframe ("the foundation goes first").

**Tone laws:** light, warm, educational, affectionate. NO pain dwelling, no enemy prosecution, no guarantee, no hard CTA, no urgency. The indictment shrinks to at most one wry aside ("the bottle doesn't exactly lead with it"). Layer 0-1 rules apply in full: FDA-clean structure-function claims, the cut never lands on her, no competitor names, no em-dashes, Natalie body rules.
