# SecondKind: The Messaging Playbook

**Version 2.0 · 2026-07-03 · Operator-approved**
For everyone writing, designing, or building for SecondKind: this is the current messaging system in one document. Everything quoted here is approved for use. If you write something new, it must pass the rules in sections 3 and 9.

---

## 1. The big idea: restoration, not transformation

Customers in every segment say the same sentence: **"I just want to feel like myself again."** Nobody asks to become someone new; they want the old one back. So we never promise transformation (the word itself is banned). We promise the return.

The brand name carries the idea: there are two kinds of you. The one who plans around a body, and the one who never had to. **We make the second kind possible again.**

The emotional headline of the whole system: **"Your comeback runs through your gut."**

## 2. Why we can promise a "better life," legally and credibly

The Bereum® 84-day human clinical study didn't just measure digestion. It measured **GI symptoms, perceived stress, and quality of life.** That citation is what licenses whole-life claims. Keep every lifestyle claim within reach of it.

## 3. The confidence doctrine (how we sound)

We are **different, not better.** Every brand says "we're great" (adjectives, free, ignored). We say what only we can say, anchored to what nobody can copy:

- The mechanism: everyone else sells inputs that are *supposed to make* the beneficial compounds. We sell the compounds.
- The patented trio (Totipro®, EpiCor®, Bereum®), each with its own human trial
- The 90-Day Challenge with a money-back guarantee bolted to the front

**The test for every new line: "Is this a claim only we can make?" If any brand could say it, cut it.**
Keep "only" claims defensible: anchor "only" to the patented trio, or carry exclusivity structurally ("We're the ones actually selling them").

## 5. The aha explainers (instant clarity, zero jargon)

Use these anywhere someone might not know what a postbiotic is:

- **"Probiotics try to make the good stuff. Postbiotics ARE the good stuff."**
- **"You wanted honey. They kept selling you bees."** (best in paid statics)
- **"The most direct support your gut can get."** (benefits header)
- Support family: "What probiotics were trying to make. Bottled." / "They send the workers. We send the work." / "Probiotics arrive hoping. Postbiotics arrive finished."
- The proof line: "About 70% of probiotic bacteria die before they ever reach your gut."

## 6. The whole-being pillar

We don't sell bloating relief. Bloating is the loudest symptom of one root:

- **"You didn't get old. Your gut got under-supplied."**
- "The 2pm crash, the sore mornings, the cold you catch every season, the moods that aren't you: one root. Your gut runs digestion, most of your immune system, and about 90% of your serotonin."
- The memory frame: "Remember when your body could take a hit? The late night, the big meal, the hard workout, and you bounced back by morning. That wasn't youth. That was a gut with full supplies."
- Supports: "Bloating is the smoke. Your gut is the fire." / "It was never just your gut. That's the point."

## 7. The 90-Day Challenge (the commitment system)

The guarantee is not fine print. It's the dare.

- The anchor: **"The longest trial behind Gut Balance ran 84 days. We round up."**
- The arc: **Days 1-30, the settling** (regularity was studied at 30 days) → **Days 31-60, the compounding** (inside the money-back window) → **Days 61-90, the forgetting** (the 84-day marks: GI, stress, quality of life)
- The dare lines: "Give us 90 days. Prove us wrong. We'll pay for the attempt." / "Three months. Then judge." / "The ask is 90 days. Days 1 through 60 are on us." / "You keep saying 'I'm not usually like this.' Give us 90 days to make that true again."
- Every CTA on site and paid: **"Take the 90-Day Challenge"**
- ⚠️ **Merchandising rule:** the guarantee covers first SINGLE bottles only. The Challenge always sells as the monthly subscription, never a 3-pack.

## 8. Premium and price

Never apologize for the price; make cheap the expensive option.

- **"The cheap option is only cheap if it works."**
- "You've probably run the cheap experiment: fifty dollars a bottle, times however many bottles, for nothing you could feel."
- "We're priced like the receipts are real. Because they are."
- Sales-call version: we could be cheaper by building it the broken way (live bacteria, big CFU number, ~70% dead on arrival). You're paying for the part the cheap version skips: three patented compounds, three human trials, third-party testing. Then close on the 90 days with the 60-day guarantee.

## 9. Voice rules (non-negotiable)

**Do:** punchy over long · empowering over clinical · instantly clear to someone with zero category knowledge · sentence case · hedged science ("about 70%," "studied over 84 days") with a receipt within reach · state the guarantee accurately (60 days, first bottle) · headers carry the argument (see section 10)

**Never:** "transformational," "life-changing," "miracle," "journey," **"gut reset"** (that's the dabbler vocabulary of the internal-shower crowd; we are a 90-day protocol, not a weekend fix) · ALL-CAPS headlines · em-dashes · rhetorical-question headlines · naming competitor brands (indict the category, never a brand) · editing customer testimonial wording (truncate only, verbatim) · promising results faster than the studies support

## 10. Landing pages: headers carry the argument

People don't read body text. They jump header to header. So: **reading only the headers must deliver the complete pitch,** and body copy is capped at 2 lines per section.

The approved page architecture (live spec: balanced-gut-2):

1. Your comeback runs through your gut. *(hero + Challenge CTA)*
2. 4.8★ from people who tried everything else first. *(proof strip, always near the top)*
3. The 90-Day Challenge starts here. *(offer)*
4. Probiotics try to make the good stuff. Postbiotics ARE the good stuff. *(aha + harvest visual)*
5. The most direct support your gut can get. *(benefit cards)*
6. You didn't get old. Your gut got under-supplied. *(whole-being)*
7. Receipts, not promises. *(92% / 89% + the three trials)*
8. The longest trial ran 84 days. We round up. *(timeline + money-back bracket)*
9. We're not a better probiotic. We're not a probiotic at all. *(comparison table)*
10. Doctors are saying yes.
11. The cheap option is only cheap if it works.
12. Three patented compounds. Nothing you'd have to google nervously.
13. 5,000+ people. Same story. *(reviews)*
14. FAQ *(questions as headers, short answers)*
15. Give it 90 days. Then judge. *(close)*

## 12. Compliance guardrails

- FDA: no disease claims (cure, treat, heal, prevent). "Supports" language stays out of headlines and lives in body/fine print. Standard disclaimer in every footer.
- Every efficacy number traces to its study (92%/89% = 30-day consumer study; the trio = their named trials).
- Testimonials are real and verbatim; truncate with ellipsis, never rewrite. No fabricated quotes, ever.
- Guarantee stated exactly: 60 days, first single bottle.
- "Only" claims: anchored to the patented trio or phrased structurally (section 3).
