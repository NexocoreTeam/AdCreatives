# Posting Cadence, How Often to Post What

This is the recommended cadence for SecondKind Bold organic content. Use it as your default weekly mix. Adjust to your bandwidth.

## The two foundational rules

1. **TikTok and IG Reels: at least 1 post per day, every day.** Sarah Levinger's research: Meta reads your posting frequency as a signal of brand quality. Inconsistent posting actively hurts your ad CPMs. Better to post a low-effort piece every day than a perfect piece every other day.
2. **Mix recipes across the week.** Same recipe every day kills recall. Same recipe never gets used breaks the recall engine. Find the rhythm in between.

---

## Weekly cadence by recipe

| Recipe | Frequency | Where it fits |
|---|---|---|
| `text-overlay-pov` (POV Short) | **Daily-ish** (3-4× per week) | Daily TikTok backbone. Low effort, ships fast; the line-testing bed. Winners graduate to bigger formats. |
| `native-ai-product-shot` | **2-3× per week** | Feed-fill on IG. Builds product recall through visual repetition. |
| `ugc-cutup-voiceover` | **1-2× per week** | When you have existing UGC footage from past campaigns. |
| `text-overlay-monologue` (Love Letter) | **1× per week** | Weekly educational charmer: callout + surprising fact + product as easy add (35-75 words). Builds warmth and brand recall. |
| `static-educational-card` | **1× per week** | Educational rhythm. IG variant or TikTok native variant, alternate weeks. |
| `not-x-objection` | **1× per week** | Brand positioning reminder. *"We're not probiotics"* style. |
| `engagement-prompt` (feed) | **1× per week MAX** | Drive comments, get VOC. More than this and the feed feels thirsty. |
| `engagement-prompt` (story) | **2-3× per week** | Polls, this-or-that, "anyone else", ephemeral, no cadence-bait risk. |
| `david-goliath-no-intro` | **1× every 1-2 weeks** | Heavier production. Save for matrix entries with strong category-failure framing. |
| `ai-ugc-influencer` | **1× per week** *(when avatar is set up)* | The recurring face/character that builds recall over time. |
| `pixar-ai-slop` | **1× per month** *(special occasions)* | When humor unlocks an objection earnest content can't. Higher effort. |

## What a typical week looks like

This is just one possible mix, you'll find your own rhythm.

| Day | Platform | Recipe | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | IG feed + TikTok | `not-x-objection` | Brand-positioning anchor for the week |
| Mon | IG story | `engagement-prompt` (poll) | Quick poll while feed engagement builds |
| Tue | TikTok | `text-overlay-pov` | Daily backbone |
| Tue | IG feed | `native-ai-product-shot` | Feed-fill brand recall |
| Wed | TikTok + IG Reels | `text-overlay-monologue` (Love Letter) | Weekly fact-led charmer |
| Wed | IG story | `engagement-prompt` (this-or-that) | Light engagement break |
| Thu | TikTok | `text-overlay-pov` | Daily backbone |
| Thu | IG carousel | `static-educational-card` | Educational rhythm |
| Fri | IG feed | `engagement-prompt` (feed post) | One weekly engagement-driver |
| Fri | TikTok | `ugc-cutup-voiceover` | UGC remix |

| Sat | TikTok + IG Reels | `ai-ugc-influencer` | Recurring-avatar episode |
| Sun | IG story | `engagement-prompt` (native poll) | Casual weekend touchpoint |

**Total for this example week:** 13 pieces (8 feed/Reels + 5 stories) across 6 different recipes.

## If you're starting smaller

Don't try to do all 13 pieces in week 1. Build up.

**Week 1-2 (starter):** 5 pieces, daily TikTok backbone (POV shorts) + 1 love letter + 1 engagement prompt
**Week 3-4 (ramp):** 8 pieces, add `native-ai-product-shot` + `static-educational-card` + a second POV
**Week 5+ (full mix):** 10-13 pieces, full weekly rhythm above

---

## Rules to respect across the cadence

- **Never two of the same recipe back-to-back.** Same recipe every day = templated feed = scroll-past. Always alternate.
- **Engagement prompts max 1×/week as feed posts.** Over-cadence kills the format.
- **Love letter is the weekly fact-led charmer.** Drop one per week minimum. Short (35-75 words), warm, educational; the piece that builds affection for the brand.
- **AI UGC influencer requires the canonical Soul ID.** If you haven't set it up, drop those from the schedule until you do.
- **Pixar AI Slop is special, not weekly.** It's high-effort and high-attention. Use it 1× per month max, when the matrix entry warrants the cringe permission.

---

## When to break cadence

- **Big launch / campaign week:** stack more love letters, AI UGC influencer pieces, and David & Goliath carousels in the lead-up. Reduce engagement prompts (the campaign IS the engagement).
- **Slow news weeks:** lean harder on POV shorts and native product shots. Lower-effort recipes carry the rhythm.
- **After an engagement prompt hits big:** ride the comment thread. Reply in SK-Bold voice over the next 24-48 hours. The comments thread is part of the content.

---

## A note on stories vs feed

Stories don't count against the "post once per day" rule, those are feed posts. Stories are bonus engagement layer. You can run 2-3 stories per day if you want (polls, behind-the-scenes, mini-engagement prompts). They expire in 24 hours so they don't clutter the feed.

Feed posts are what Meta and TikTok use to measure your brand-quality signal. Those are the ones you must hit daily.
