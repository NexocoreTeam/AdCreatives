# TikTok Love Letter (organic)

**Output:** video · **Platforms:** TikTok + IG Reels · **Lift:** low · **Brand-intro:** final beat (~60-75% in)

> **Format redefined 2026-06-11 from operator-approved examples.** The old version of this recipe described a 150-300 word confessional monologue. That is NOT a TikTok love letter. The spec below is the correct one; full reference examples + 8 worked SK Bold scripts live at `clients/secondkind-bold/copy/tiktok-love-letters-v3.md`.

## What this is

A short, warm, educational direct-address piece: **35-75 words, 3 beats, 20-40 seconds.** Text overlay revealed beat-by-beat over silent B-roll, or spoken as VO by the AI UGC avatar. The text is the hero; the video is visual rhythm.

**The 3 beats:**

1. **Callout + hook fact.** Affectionate direct address ("Girls," "Hot girls," "Guys," "Ladies") plus ONE surprising, hedged fact or identity behavior. Hedge the science: "around," "about," "appears to."
2. **The education.** 2-3 emoji-led, ingredient-level lines in structure-function language ("X supports Y"). Receipts said casually ("studied over 84 days"). Compound names (EpiCor, Bereum, Totipro) live here, never in the hook.
3. **Product + effortless close.** Brand named once, then a low-key close ("two capsules a day," "easy add") and optionally one smart kicker reframe ("the foundation goes first").

**Tone:** light, warm, educational, affectionate. NO pain dwelling, no enemy prosecution, no guarantee, no hard CTA, no urgency. At most one wry aside ("the bottle doesn't exactly lead with it"). FDA structure-function phrasing only.

## When to use it

- Unaware / problem-aware personas who respond to a surprising fact
- Identity-badge opens ("hot girls...") for Danielle and Paula
- Any matrix entry with a clean ingredient-to-function education beat

## Worked example (script 2 from the v3 set)

**Beat 1 (opener, full-screen):**

> *Girls, your gut lining rebuilds itself about every week. What you feed it today actually matters.*

**Beat 2 (emoji-led reveals):**

> *Postbiotics are compounds your gut uses for exactly that:*
> *😌 Lining support*
> *🚽 Regularity*
> *⚡ Steady energy*

**Beat 3 (product + close):**

> *Gut Balance packs three clinically studied ones into two capsules a day. Easy add.*

**Caption:** about every week. let that sink in.
**Pinned comment:** three compounds, three human studies (30-day, 84-day, 12-week).

## How to produce the visuals in Higgsfield

3-4 quiet, faceless lifestyle clips behind the text. Generate in one session for tone consistency.

**IMPORTANT:** Higgsfield produces **clean visuals only, no text baked in.** All text is added in CapCut.

```
1. "Shot on iPhone, candid. Hands stirring oatmeal in a cream stoneware bowl, golden morning light through a kitchen window, slightly imperfect framing, no faces visible, 9:16 vertical, shallow depth of field. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."

2. "Shot on iPhone, candid. A small handful of pill capsules in an open palm, kitchen counter background, soft morning light, no face, 9:16 vertical. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."

3. "Shot on iPhone, candid. Glass of water being filled at a kitchen sink, midday natural light, slightly off-center composition, no faces, 9:16 vertical. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."

4. "Shot on iPhone, candid. Amber glass supplement jar on a tidy kitchen counter next to keys and a phone, evening light, no faces, 9:16 vertical. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."
```

**Tips:** keep "Shot on iPhone, candid" in every prompt; reject stock-photo-looking outputs; never include faces (the text is the focal point); ALWAYS keep the no-text clause.

### Production workflow

1. Pick the matrix entry / fact angle (or pull a script from the v3 set)
2. Write or adapt the 3 beats (35-75 words total)
3. Generate 3-4 B-roll clips in Higgsfield
4. CapCut: beat-by-beat text reveal, large centered text, minimal ambient music
5. Export 9:16; post with caption + pinned-comment receipt; reply to 5+ comments in the first hour

### Pitfalls

- Writing it long. If it passes 75 words it has become a different format. Cut.
- Dwelling in pain or prosecuting the category. This recipe is the warm one; the expose recipes carry the indictment.
- Leading with a compound name. The hook is the fact; the compounds are beat 2.
- Unhedged claims. "Around," "about," "studied over X days" keep it credible and compliant.
- Brand entering before the final beat.
- B-roll with faces or dramatic wellness aesthetics. Quiet and domestic only.
