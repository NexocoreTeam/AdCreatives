# David & Goliath (organic, brand-late)

**Output:** video OR image carousel · **Platforms:** TikTok + IG Reels + IG Carousel · **Lift:** medium · **Brand-intro:** ~70% in

## What this is

Call out an enemy, an industry, a scam, a broken belief, and contrast it with the differentiator. The brand stays invisible until the broken model has been fully discredited. Video form works if founder footage exists; static carousel works as the fallback.

## When to use it

- Solution-aware or product-aware personas (they need to know what they're rejecting)
- Matrix positions with explicit competitive-pain framing
- Briefs where `competitive-gaps.md` documents specific category problems
- When you have founder/anti-category footage in the agency library

⚠️ **The Goliath must be a BROKEN BELIEF or CATEGORY PROBLEM, never a named competitor.** Legal risk + drifts into brand-bashing.

## Worked example, Danielle bloating brief

**Brief:** `secondkind-bold-gut-balance-0c7dc7.yaml`
**The Goliath:** the probiotic industry's survival-gauntlet model
**Persona:** Done-Everything Danielle

### The content (video version, 60-second VO)

**Beats 1-4, Establish the Goliath (no brand mentioned)**

> *The probiotic industry is built on a lie.*
> *They sell you live bacteria. Tell you it'll fix your gut.*
> *They don't tell you that 90% of those bacteria die in your stomach acid before they ever reach your gut.*
> *The ones that survive face a colonization battle they were never built to win.*

**Beats 5-7, Show the consequences (still no brand)**

> *Which is why you can take a $50 probiotic every morning for six months and feel exactly nothing.*
> *That's not a problem with one brand. That's the entire delivery model.*
> *And nobody's going to fix it because the model sells.*

**Beat 8, The reveal**

> *Unless you stop sending bacteria.*
> *And send the compounds your gut was supposed to get from those bacteria directly.*
> *That's what we do. Gut Balance is postbiotics. Already active. No survival gauntlet.*

**Visual (PRIMARY):** Founder talking head + B-roll cutaways (probiotic bottles, stomach acid demo, lab footage). Use the agency's existing founder library.

**Visual (FALLBACK, carousel version):** 7 slides, editorial type treatment. Slide 1: "The probiotic industry is built on a lie." Slide 7: brand reveal.

### Production workflow (video)

1. Pull founder footage from agency library
2. Draft 60-second VO from the brief's competitive frame + `competitive-gaps.md`
3. Record or AI-voice the VO
4. Cut in CapCut/Premiere with B-roll
5. Export 9:16

### Pitfalls

- Naming a competitor (Seed, Ritual, etc.), replace with category language ("the conventional probiotic model")
- Brand intro before the 60% mark, the format collapses
- Tone drifts into brand-bashing, reframe to "industry mechanics" not "those brands are bad"
- Founder reads like a sales pitch, should sound like someone explaining why they got into this, not pitching
