# UGC Cut-up + AI Voiceover

**Output:** video · **Platforms:** TikTok + IG Reels · **Lift:** low · **Brand-intro:** mid-to-late

## What this is

Take existing UGC footage you already own (originally shot for another campaign), strip the audio, recut as A-roll/B-roll, and layer a new AI voiceover that addresses a different matrix pain point. The creator becomes anonymous lifestyle B-roll. Voiceover delivers the new monologue.

## When to use it

- You have a backlog of UGC from prior campaigns
- Matrix positions different from what the UGC was originally produced for
- High-volume weekly cadence, recombines existing assets, basically infinite remix potential
- The original UGC's mood/setting matches the new pain point (don't force gym footage onto a sleep pain)

⚠️ **Recipe requires existing UGC.** If none available, default to `text-overlay-monologue`.

## Worked example, Danielle bloating brief

**Brief:** `secondkind-bold-gut-balance-0c7dc7.yaml`
**Original UGC source:** assume existing footage of a previous creator in her kitchen, drinking coffee, putting on shoes
**New pain target:** end-of-day bloating (same brief, but the UGC was shot for "morning energy")

### The content (75-second VO over recut UGC)

**Beats and visual mapping:**

| VO line | Visual (existing UGC, no creator audio) |
|---|---|
| *"There's a moment around 7pm…"* | Creator's hands closing a laptop |
| *"…where your jeans don't fit and you don't know why."* | Creator pulling on jeans (close-up, no face) |
| *"You ate clean today. You walked at lunch. You did everything."* | Creator chopping vegetables, then a salad bowl |
| *"And by dinner your body has staged a coup."* | Creator sitting on a couch, low light |
| *"So you go back to googling 'why am I bloated' for the 400th time."* | Hand on a phone, screen blurred |
| *"You've taken three probiotics in two years. You feel exactly nothing."* | Hand opening a medicine cabinet |
| [BRAND INTRO ~55s, ~75% mark] | Slow push-in on the Gut Balance bottle on a kitchen counter |
| *"What if the model is wrong. What if you shouldn't be taking bacteria at all."* | Cut between bottle and creator's hand reaching toward it |
| *"What if you should be taking what bacteria make."* | Creator pours from bottle to palm |

**AI voiceover spec:**
- Voice: ElevenLabs or similar, female 30-40, warm but tired, conversational
- Pace: slow, considered, like she's thinking out loud, not reading copy
- Music: minimal, low ambient pad. Drop entirely under the brand reveal

### Production workflow

1. Pull UGC clips matching the mood (kitchen, evening, hands-not-face) from agency library
2. Write VO script from brief's PASTOR copy, adapted
3. Generate AI VO in ElevenLabs
4. Cut visual to VO in CapCut/Premiere
5. Add ambient track + small text accents
6. Export 9:16

### Pitfalls

- Using clips where the creator is talking on-camera, their mouth movement betrays the voiceover. Hands/body/setting only for the emotional beats.
- Mood mismatch, gym UGC for a sleep pain feels wrong. Match emotional register.
- AI VO sounds like a reader, direct ElevenLabs to "conversational, thinking out loud." Listen back, regenerate if it's too polished.
- Brand intro too early, wait for the pain to fully land before the bottle appears.
- Crediting the original creator, don't. The repurposing is fine under your license, but you don't need to attribute on the recut.
