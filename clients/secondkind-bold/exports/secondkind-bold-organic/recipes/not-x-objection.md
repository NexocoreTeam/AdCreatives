# We're not [X]

**Output:** image OR short video · **Platforms:** Instagram + TikTok · **Lift:** very_low · **Brand-intro:** none (brand IS the message; X comes first)

## What this is

Brand-positioning objection-handling format. Lead with what the brand is NOT, then justify with proof in ≤3 lines. The X must be a CATEGORY, not a competitor.

**Default visual:** native AI phone-snap setting background with text overlaid. Plain Snow White editorial is the occasional alternative, see below.

## When to use it

- Solution-aware or product-aware personas (they need to know the category language)
- Brands with a clear category they're rejecting (postbiotics not probiotics, etc.)
- Matrix positions where the differentiator IS the entire pitch
- Fast feed-fill when you need brand-positioning content this week

## Worked example, Danielle bloating brief

**Brief:** `secondkind-bold-gut-balance-0c7dc7.yaml`
**Category being rejected:** probiotics
**Format:** static IG square (1080×1080)

### THE TEXT (VA adds in Canva)

**Headline (Caslon Condensed, large):**
> *We're not probiotics.*
> *We don't want to be.*

**Body (Neue Montreal, smaller, 3 lines):**

> The ones that do don't colonize.

**Bottom-right corner:** SecondKind wordmark only. No CTA.

### THE VISUAL, occasional alternative (Snow White editorial)

When the moment calls for premium editorial (brand announcement, press moment, anniversary post):

- Plain Snow White background (#fefcf6)
- Same Caslon + Neue Montreal type treatment
- Single yellow accent on mechanism word
- Editorial Minimalism, generous whitespace
- SK wordmark bottom-right

Use this maybe 1 in 4 times. Default to phone-snap for most posts.

### Production workflow

**Default path (phone-snap):**
1. Lift the 3 body lines from the brief's `benefit_callouts` field
2. Generate phone-snap background in Higgsfield
3. Open Canva "IG Square Native" template, drop background
4. Drop headline + body in clear area of photo, Caslon + Neue Montreal
5. Add yellow accent on key word, SK wordmark bottom-right
6. Export 1080×1080 PNG

**Editorial path (Snow White alternative):**
1. Lift body lines
2. Open Canva "IG Square Editorial" template
3. Drop text, apply typography
4. Add yellow accent + wordmark
5. Export

### Pitfalls

- "We're not [competitor name]", illegal-adjacent, drifts into bashing. ALWAYS use category language.
- Body copy too long, strictly ≤3 lines, ≤30 words total
- Adding a CTA, kills the organic feel. Just brand mark in corner.
- Multiple accent colors, single yellow accent only
- Plain Snow White every time, too "ad"-feeling for organic. Mix in phone-snap backgrounds.
- Photo background too busy, text gets lost. Regenerate with more negative space.
