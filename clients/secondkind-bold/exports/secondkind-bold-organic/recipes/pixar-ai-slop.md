# Pixar AI Slop (organic)

**Output:** video OR image series · **Platforms:** TikTok + IG Reels · **Lift:** medium · **Brand-intro:** mid · **Skill:** special_vfx

## What this is

Outrageous AI-animated character ad. Visibly AI, never trying to look real. Permission granted to be cringe because the audience can see it's not a person. Best for personifying body parts, conditions, or category villains.

## When to use it

- VA has AI video/animation skills (Higgsfield, Runway, Pika, etc.)
- Pain points where literal UGC would feel preachy or self-serious
- Personification opportunities, the gut, the bloat, the probiotic that didn't make it
- Humor unlocks objections earnest copy can't reach

⚠️ **Do not recommend this if the VA can't make it yet.** Flag the skill requirement.

## Worked example, Danielle bloating brief

**Brief:** `secondkind-bold-gut-balance-0c7dc7.yaml`
**The character set:** Two probiotics ("Pro" and "Biotic") and a postbiotic ("Posty")

### The content (30-60 second video)

**Scene 1**, Pro and Biotic, two anthropomorphized cartoon bacteria, in a long pill-shaped capsule. They're nervous, holding tiny backpacks.

> *PRO: "Okay so we just have to survive the stomach. Then the small intestine. Then convince an entire ecosystem to let us in. Easy."*
> *BIOTIC: "How many of us are in this bottle?"*
> *PRO: "Ten billion."*
> *BIOTIC: "And how many usually make it?"*
> *PRO: "...don't worry about it."*

**Scene 2**, A wave of (clearly AI-rendered) stomach acid washes over them. They scream cartoon screams. Most disappear in puffs of pixel dust.

**Scene 3**, Two survivors emerge into the intestine. They see a thriving city of resident bacteria looking at them like strangers.

> *PRO: "...nobody's expecting us, are they."*

**Scene 4: brand intro, ~60% mark**, Cut to Posty, an obviously different character (different shape, glowing). She's just walking around the gut city. Nobody's mad. She's just delivering.

> *VO: "Postbiotics skip the survival part. You send the compounds, not the messengers."*

**Visual style:** Pixar-ified but obviously AI. Slightly off proportions. Reuse the same three characters for every episode, that's the recall engine.

### Production workflow

1. Generate character refs (Pro, Biotic, Posty) in Higgsfield Soul-ID or similar (one-time)
2. Write 4-scene script
3. Generate each scene in Higgsfield/Runway with character continuity
4. Edit + add VO in CapCut
5. Export 9:16

### Pitfalls

- Trying to make characters look realistic, kills the format. Lean into cringe AI.
- New characters every episode, destroys recall. Reuse the same three.
- Brand intro too early, wait for the survival failure to land first
- Treating the body as gross, characters should be cute/sympathetic even when failing
