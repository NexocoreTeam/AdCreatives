# Native AI Product Shot

**Output:** image OR carousel · **Platforms:** Instagram + TikTok slideshow · **Lift:** very_low · **Brand-intro:** none (product is the image; caption is native)

## What this is

AI-generated product photo styled to look like a phone snap in a real setting, kitchen counter, gym bag, bedside table, coffee table. Native-looking caption, sounds like a friend's post, not a brand. Lean into imperfection: natural light, casual angle, real-world clutter at the edges.

## When to use it

- Product-aware personas who already know the brand
- Matrix positions where lifestyle context sells the product
- Feed-fill content between bigger campaign pieces
- Building brand recall through visual repetition (one of these every 3 posts)

## How to produce the visuals in Higgsfield

Use **Higgsfield Product Photoshoot** mode (the dedicated product photography feature). Drag your Gut Balance reference photo in, then paste prompts.

**IMPORTANT:** Higgsfield should produce **clean product photos only, no text overlays, no captions baked into the image.** The product's own label is the only text in the image. The native caption ("the only thing I added this month") is added separately when posting.

**Paste these into Higgsfield Product Photoshoot, generate 1:1 or 4:5 for IG, 9:16 for TikTok slideshow:**

**Tips for Higgsfield:**
- Always include "amber glass jar", matches the brand visual identity
- NEVER use "professional photography" or "studio lighting", breaks the native feel
- Add "slightly imperfect framing" or "candid" to every prompt
- Generate 3-4 variations per scene, pick the most "found"-looking one
- Reject any output that looks art-directed, natural-window-light + slight clutter wins
- For TikTok slideshow, change `1:1 aspect ratio` to `9:16 vertical` in the prompts
- The product's own label IS the only text in the image, every other text/caption is added when posting

### Pitfalls

- Image looks art-directed, kills the format. If it looks like a campaign, regenerate.
- Multiple products in frame, focus on one. Other items are scene-setting only.
- Studio lighting / ring light, gives away the AI. Use natural-window-light prompts.
- Caption with hashtags or CTA, breaks the "friend's post" illusion.
- Too clean a scene, add small clutter (toast crumbs, slightly wrinkled napkin) so it reads as lived-in.
- Same exact scene twice, vary the setting; same bottle, different life context.
