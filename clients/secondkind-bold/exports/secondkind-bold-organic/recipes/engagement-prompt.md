# Engagement Prompt

**Output:** image / carousel / native poll · **Platforms:** Instagram (post, story) + TikTok (post, story) · **Lift:** very_low · **Brand-intro:** none (brand voice IS the signal)

## What this is

Image, carousel, or platform-native poll designed to drive **comments, replies, or taps**. Asks the audience for a number, single word, binary choice, or quick rank. Never asks for reflection or vulnerability.

**Default visual:** native AI phone-snap setting background with the question overlaid in clear space. Plain Snow White editorial is the occasional alternative (used when text needs maximum legibility, variants 5, 7).

## The core rule, low friction, voice intact

The bar for replying must be LOWER than the bar for liking. If the answer requires more than 3 seconds or types more than a few words, the prompt is a journaling exercise, not engagement.

**The brand voice lives in the ASSUMPTION baked into the question, not in the difficulty.**

| ✅ Low friction + voice intact | ❌ Too high-effort |
|---|---|
| *"How many probiotics have you tried?"* (assumes multiple) | *"Name a symptom you've quietly accepted as normal."* |
| *"Anyone else's gut hate Sundays?"* | *"Tell me a wellness lie you used to believe."* |
| *"Bloated by lunch or bloated by dinner?"* | *"What's a gut symptom you wish you could talk about?"* |

## When to use it

- Weekly feed-rhythm break between value-delivery content
- Engagement metrics are lagging
- You want VOC, the comments ARE research material for future copy
- Building social-proof momentum before a campaign push

## When NOT to use it

- More than 1× per week as a feed post (kills the format)
- Open confessional questions (memoir, not engagement)
- Anything that asks to "name a brand" (legal risk + drifts into bashing, stay on categories)
- Wellness-influencer cheer ("drop a 💚 if...")

## The 7 variants, visuals + workflows

### Variant 1: Number / quick-answer (strongest pattern)

Answer is a single number or short word. Lowest possible friction.

**Examples:**
- *"How many probiotics have you tried?"*
- *"How many supplements are in your bathroom right now?"*
- *"How many days into a probiotic before you give up?"*
- *"What time does the bloat hit?"*
- *"How many times have you Googled 'why am I bloated' this month?"*

**Default visual:** phone-snap photo background with the question overlaid in negative space.

**Higgsfield prompt (rotate across posts):**
```
"Shot on iPhone, candid. A medicine cabinet open, lined with multiple supplement bottles, casual arrangement, soft bathroom light, large negative space in the upper portion of the frame, slightly imperfect framing, 1:1 aspect ratio. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."
```

Question text (Caslon Condensed, large) sits in the upper negative space. SK wordmark bottom-right.

**Other backgrounds to rotate:**
- Kitchen counter with empty supplement bottles
- A drawer half-open with supplement bottles inside
- A nightstand with a pill organizer

**Workflow:** Higgsfield → Canva → post = total.

### Variant 2: "Anyone else..." commiserating

Implies shared experience. Easy yes/comment reply.

**Examples:**
- *"Anyone else's gut hate Sundays?"*
- *"Anyone else have a 4pm energy cliff?"*
- *"Anyone else's stomach has a curfew?"*
- *"Anyone else feel fine in the morning and 3 months pregnant by dinner?"*

**Default visual:** phone-snap photo background matching the mood.

**Higgsfield prompts (match to the question):**
```
For Sunday/4pm energy:
"Shot on iPhone, candid. A couch corner in soft afternoon light, an open laptop closed shut, a half-empty coffee mug, negative space in the upper portion, 1:1 aspect ratio. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."

For evening bloat:
"Shot on iPhone, candid. A pair of denim jeans folded on a bed, soft evening light, negative space in the upper portion of the frame, lived-in bedroom in soft focus, 1:1 aspect ratio. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."
```

**Workflow:** total.

### Variant 3: This-or-that (binary)

Pick one. Either side is a valid answer.

**Examples:**
- *"Bloated by lunch or bloated by dinner?"*
- *"Probiotic person or postbiotic person?"*
- *"Capsules or powders?"*
- *"Morning supplements or evening?"*

**Default for STATIC feed post:** phone-snap photo background, question overlaid in clear space.

**Default for STORY POLL:** open IG/TikTok app, add poll sticker, type question. No design tool needed.

**Higgsfield prompt (static):**
```
"Shot on iPhone, candid. A kitchen counter at morning, soft natural light, two coffee mugs side by side (one full, one empty), negative space above, 1:1 aspect ratio. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."
```

**Workflow:** (static) or (story poll).

### Variant 4: Native polls (Story)

Platform-native poll sticker. Zero-friction tap-to-vote. **No Higgsfield needed, use what's already in your story or a quick AI background.**

**Examples:**
- *"Have you ever finished a 90-day supply of probiotics?"* → Yes / No / I keep starting over
- *"How long before you cancel a supplement that's not working?"* → 30 / 60 / 90 / I just keep paying
- *"What does your gut hate most?"* → Dairy / Gluten / Stress / Mystery

**Workflow:** Open IG Story or TikTok Story, add native poll sticker, type.

### Variant 5: Two-truths-and-a-lie

Pick the lie. Three-second decision, comment one number.

**Example:**
> *"Two of these are true about probiotics. One is propaganda. Pick the lie:*
> *1. Most probiotic bacteria die in stomach acid*
> *2. Taking them with food improves survival*
> *3. Live cultures must colonize to do anything"*

**Default visual: PLAIN background** (Snow White or off-white). Text density is too high for legible phone-snap overlay.

This is one of the explicit Snow-White-default variants. Keep it clean for legibility.

**Workflow:** Skip Higgsfield. Canva "IG Square Editorial" template, drop 3 statements + framing question.

### Variant 6: Tier-list invitation

Rank in the comments. People love ranking.

**Example:**
> *"Rank these from least to most useful:*
> *• Probiotics • Kombucha • Fiber gummies • ACV shots • Postbiotics • 'Just drink water'"*

**Default visual:** phone-snap photo of a relevant scene (shelf of supplements, drawer of products), tier-list text overlaid in clear space.

**Higgsfield prompt:**
```
"Shot on iPhone, candid. A bathroom shelf with multiple supplement bottles and powder containers casually arranged, soft natural light, large negative space on the left side of the frame for text, 1:1 aspect ratio. No text, no captions, no overlays, no graphics, no typography."
```

### Variant 7: Carousel quiz / diagnostic

Self-identifying quiz, sticky format, low friction (just swipe).

**Example:**
> *"Which probiotic excuse is yours? →"*
> Slide 2: *"They didn't survive your stomach acid"*
> Slide 3: *"They survived but didn't colonize"*
> Slide 4: *"You stopped at month 2 because nothing was happening"*
> Slide 5: *"All of the above. Postbiotics skip all of it."*

**Default visual: MIXED.** Slides 1 + 5 (hook + reveal) can use phone-snap backgrounds. Slides 2-4 (the answer options) work best on **plain backgrounds** for legibility, they have more text density.

OR: keep all 5 slides plain Snow White for consistency, this is one of the variants where editorial-default is fine.

**Workflow:** Canva carousel template, 5 slides.

## Worked example, week of engagement priming before a campaign

You're shipping the *"We're not probiotics"* campaign next Monday. Want engagement priming the week before.

| Day | Variant | Visual default | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** (week before) | Variant 1 (number) | Phone-snap | IG square: medicine cabinet photo + *"How many probiotics have you tried?"* overlaid in upper space |
| **Wed** | Variant 3 (this-or-that) | None needed | IG Story poll: *"Bloated by lunch or bloated by dinner?"* with native poll sticker |
| **Fri** | Variant 5 (two-truths) | Plain Snow White | IG square, 3 statements (legibility wins over native here) |

## Cadence rule

| Surface | Max frequency |
|---|---|
| Feed posts | **1× per week** |
| Stories | 2-3× per week |

If you want more engagement than this allows, the answer is **better value content**, not more engagement bait.

---

## Pitfalls

- **Question requires reflection or 10+ words to answer.** Journaling prompt. Re-pick.
- **Wellness-influencer cheer.** *"Drop a 🤢", "share your journey", "comment YES"*, off-brand.
- **Asking audiences to name competitors.** Legal risk + brand-bashing drift. Stay on categories and behaviors.
- **Over-cadence.** More than 1×/week feed posts kills the format.
- **Voice gets sacrificed to chase engagement.** Answerable in 3 seconds AND still sounds like SK-Bold. Voice in the assumption, not in difficulty.
- **No SK wordmark.** Even though brand isn't the message, a small wordmark in corner makes engaged followers find the profile.
- **Plain Snow White by default for all variants.** Too "ad"-feeling, only use plain backgrounds for variants 5 and (optionally) 7. Default to phone-snap for variants 1, 2, 3, 6.
- **No follow-up engagement.** When followers comment, reply in the brand voice. The comments thread is part of the content.
