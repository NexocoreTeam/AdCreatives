**Hook angle:** Fifty dollars a month. Two months. Back to zero. You know this story.

**Tactic:** sunk-cost-mirror

**Story / setup:** Carousel or video: Opens on a bank statement-style graphic showing two $50 charges labeled 'Probiotic Subscription' — no brand named — followed by a cancellation. Text: 'Month one: hopeful. Month two: nothing. Month three: canceled.' Beat. 'The plateau isn't bad luck. It's the mechanism ceiling of live-culture delivery.' Next frame: postbiotic mechanism in two sentences. Final frame: 'No plateau. Because it's not a probiotic.' CTA with first-order offer.

**Why it works:** Pain-001 is the dominant churn pattern. Temporal_discounting activates when past spend is made viscerally visible — the sunk-cost is named before the solution arrives, which sharpens willingness to try something categorically different.

**Format fit:** carousel, promotion-discount

**Static treatment:** Minimalist statement-style graphic. Three lines stacked: '$50. Month one.' / '$50. Month two.' / 'Canceled. Month three.' Below a thin rule: 'The plateau was always built in.' Product and CTA below. No photography. Monochrome with single accent color on 'Canceled.'

**Psychology trigger:** temporal_discounting
