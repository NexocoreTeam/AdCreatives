# SecondKind Messaging Overlay

Date: 2026-07-09
Source: SecondKind new messaging.docx

## Executive Take

Phase-one research remains valid. The ICPs, pain points, probiotic fatigue, competitor gaps, and postbiotic mechanism all still matter.

The new messaging does not replace that work. It changes the hierarchy:

1. Gut health is the daily foundation.
2. Postbiotics are the technology.
3. The offer is a simple 90-day reset routine.
4. The brand should feel like an essential wellness system, not another gut supplement.

## What Stays

- Keep the core ICPs:
  - Done-Everything Danielle
  - Immune-Anxious Isaac
  - New-Normal New Mom Natalie
  - Functional-Curious Practitioner Paul
  - Burnout Biohacker Brandon
  - Perimenopause Paula
- Keep the pain map:
  - bloating
  - irregularity
  - brain fog
  - low energy
  - poor sleep
  - stress and feeling off
  - probiotic fatigue
  - skepticism around supplement claims
- Keep the competitor gap:
  - live-bacteria approaches create a survival/activation problem
  - postbiotics deliver the useful compounds directly
  - do not name competitors in customer-facing copy
- Keep the brand voice:
  - premium
  - restrained
  - clinically literate
  - direct
  - no hype
  - no miracle language

## What Changes

### Positioning

Old center of gravity:

"A premium postbiotic supplement for bloating, digestion, and gut-brain balance."

New center of gravity:

"A postbiotic-first daily wellness system that supports the gut, the foundation behind how the whole body feels."

This makes SecondKind more must-have. The gut is no longer framed as one wellness category among many. It becomes the system behind everyday function.

### Copy Hierarchy

Use this order in most cold acquisition:

1. Name the real-life feeling.
2. Reframe it as a gut-foundation issue.
3. Explain that probiotics are the process and postbiotics are the payoff.
4. Present SecondKind as the daily routine.
5. Push the 90-day reset or simple daily consistency offer.

### Offer Strategy

The strongest acquisition offer is now:

"The Real-Life 90-Day Gut Reset"

Core components:

- 3 bottles of Gut Balance
- 90-day reset guide
- simple "what to expect" timeline
- bloating/trigger tracker
- gut-friendly meal or snack prompts
- weekly reset tips or SMS/email check-ins
- travel/routine tips
- guidance for missed days
- money-back 90-day guarantee if approved by client/legal

Use this as a value stack, not just a discount. "Buy 2, get 1 free" should support the reset logic, not lead as a coupon.

### Strategic Promise

Master promise:

"Support the system behind how you feel every day: your gut."

Everything should ladder back to this. Bloating and digestion are the entry points. Whole-body rhythm is the larger promise.

## New Copy Rules

### Lead With Daily Non-Negotiable Logic

Useful structures:

- "Your gut works every day. Support it every day."
- "Gut health is not optional."
- "You do not wait until your mouth hurts to brush your teeth. Do not wait until your gut feels off to support it."
- "Most people do not need another random supplement. They need a routine they can stick with."

### Make The 90-Day Frame Concrete

Use:

- "One bottle can start the change. Three months gives your body the reset window."
- "Days 1-30: Start the routine."
- "Days 31-60: Build better rhythm."
- "Days 61-90: Make it stick."

Avoid pretending one bottle completes the whole transformation. The more durable story is consistency.

### Use Postbiotics As The Payoff

Approved idea:

"Probiotics are the process. Postbiotics are the payoff."

Support lines:

- "Skip the middleman."
- "Stop chasing bacteria. Start supporting balance."
- "Postbiotics are the beneficial compounds good bacteria are meant to create."

### Elevate The Product Without Overclaiming

Say:

- "supports digestion"
- "supports regularity"
- "supports gut lining"
- "supports immune resilience"
- "supports calm, sleep quality, stress response, focus, and emotional balance"
- "helps you feel lighter, clearer, and more in rhythm"

Avoid:

- "controls mood"
- "fixes sleep"
- "treats anxiety"
- "cures brain fog"
- "eliminates bloating"
- "guaranteed results"

## Paid Creative Implications

### Cold Hooks

- "Bloating, sluggishness, or feeling off-routine? Start with your gut."
- "A gut reset for real life, not another complicated wellness protocol."
- "Give your gut 90 days of consistent support."
- "The easiest gut routine is the one you will actually stick with."
- "Your daily gut reset, simplified."
- "Your gut works every day. Support it every day."

### Landing Page Hero Direction

Headline:

"Your gut does not need another quick fix. It needs 90 days of consistency."

Subhead:

"The Real-Life 90-Day Gut Reset gives you three months of daily gut support plus simple tools to help you build a routine that supports digestion, regularity, and everyday balance."

Support:

"No complicated protocol. No restrictive meal plan. No wellness overwhelm."

CTA:

"Start your 90-day reset"

Secondary CTA:

"See what is included"

### Static Ad Direction

The "elite product" signal should come from:

- restraint
- clarity
- premium editorial layout
- clinical proof rows
- system-level framing
- 90-day routine value stack

It should not come from:

- luxury-for-luxury's-sake copy
- exaggerated exclusivity
- all-caps
- scarcity
- aggressive challenger copy

### UGC / Creator Direction

Creator-native ads can stay real and routine-based, but the spoken/written angle should shift from "I found a better probiotic" to:

- "I stopped treating gut health like a random fix."
- "I gave my gut 90 days of consistency."
- "This is the easiest gut routine I have actually stuck with."
- "I did not need a complicated protocol. I needed a routine."

## Strategy Going Forward

The brand should now be treated as a category-creation play plus a habit-building offer.

Use phase-one research to identify the pain and skepticism. Use the new messaging to raise the stakes:

- Pain: "I am bloated, foggy, sluggish, irregular, and off."
- Old interpretation: "I need a gut supplement."
- New interpretation: "My gut foundation is not being supported consistently."
- Product answer: "Postbiotic-first daily support."
- Offer answer: "The Real-Life 90-Day Gut Reset."

This gives SecondKind a more defensible lane: not a symptom pill, not a probiotic alternative only, but a daily foundation product for whole-body wellness.
