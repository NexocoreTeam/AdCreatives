## Color palette (authoritative HEX — overrides any vision-extracted values)

### Main colors (anchor shades)

| Name | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Taupe | `#ddc7ac` | Grounded neutral — stability, transparency, approachability |
| Soft Blush | `#fcc9bb` | Gentle, uplifting — femininity, warmth, connection |
| Vivid Coral | `#f15222` | Bold accent — energy, conversation, creativity (use sparingly) |
| Charcoal | `#4f463d` | Depth, contrast, sophistication — body text |

### Supporting colors

| Name | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Cream | `#f9f3eb` | Backgrounds, breathing space |
| Soft Taupe | `#efdac8` | Lighter, airier complement to Taupe |
| Sky Blue | `#cee2f5` | Openness, peace |
| Soft Blue | `#97b0c4` | Trust, calm, balance |
| Muted Coral | `#f78469` | Softer counterpart to Vivid Coral |

### Usage rules from the doc

- **Vivid Coral = accent only.** Buttons, key graphics, CTAs. Don't dominate with it.
- **Taupe / Soft Blush / Cream = backgrounds + large surfaces.**
- **Charcoal = body text + outlines.**
- **Avoid:** Charcoal + Taupe alone (no contrast — needs Cream or Soft Taupe buffer); Sky Blue + Soft Blue alone (washed out — needs warm tone).
- **Reserve Charcoal for body text** — Taupe / Soft Blush fail WCAG as text.
- **High-contrast CTA recipes:** Vivid Coral on Cream, OR Charcoal on Muted Coral.

## Visual identity / illustrations

- Hand-drawn line style, modern, warm — illustrations include: flowers (growth), megaphone + location flag (community), dove + prayer hands (faith), gallery wall + door + shopper bag (retail story).
- Patterns: organic abstract shapes, subtle pinstripes (vertical, soft blue), tile-inspired grid (coral + blush).
- Brand marks include: pennant ("SAVED BY GRACE CO. — CHARLOTTE NC"), banner ribbons ("YOU BELONG HERE — SBG CO."), circular submarks ("for you, your home & your people").

## Tone

- Warm, encouraging, faith-forward, family-centered, authentic, approachable.
- "Like a friend sharing something meaningful — never preachy."
- Inclusive ("Faith-Rooted, Not Faith-Restricted").
- Empowering — uplifts women "as they walk in purpose."
