# Competitive Gap Map — Saved by Grace Co. (SBG Co.)

**TL;DR:** SBG Co.'s most defensible white space is the intersection of durable faith-forward product quality and intentional mom-and-mini coordinate merchandising — a combination no competitor in this set replicates. Kerusso owns message credibility but fails on fabric durability; Elevated Faith matches the premium quality story but has a broken trust infrastructure; SpearmintLOVE owns the baby/kids aesthetic niche but has no values clarity and chronic sizing failures; Simply Southern is a lifestyle brand cosplaying as a faith brand. SBG Co.'s made-to-order model, 10-year founder-led community loyalty, and multi-lane message architecture (faith, mom humor, patriotic, lifestyle) give it the strongest product story in the category — the primary creative task is converting that product truth into paid angles that turn the 14-day wait from a friction point into a proof of craft, and that position the brand's values clarity as the answer to the trust vacuums SpearmintLOVE and Simply Southern have left open among faith-driven moms.

## Exploitable Gaps

### Apparel durability — competitors' products fade, stretch, and deteriorate quickly
- **Competitors failing:** Kerusso
- **Evidence:** "Unfortunately the quality of the tshirts are horrible. They fade super quickly and the collar stretches out after one to two uses. I spent over $150 on these shirts and feel super bummed about it."
- **Our advantage:** SBG Co.'s made-to-order model — produced in-house in Charlotte, NC rather than mass-manufactured overseas — is structurally designed for consistent per-unit quality control. Each piece is made individually, not pulled from bulk inventory, which eliminates the race-to-bottom fabric sourcing that produces Kerusso's fading and collar collapse.
- **Ad angle:** Your faith shouldn't fade in the wash. SBG Co. pieces are made one at a time, in Charlotte, NC — because something you wear to declare what you believe deserves to last longer than two uses.

### Mom-and-mini coordinating apparel — competitors offer family coverage but not intentional coordinate merchandising
- **Competitors failing:** Kerusso, Elevated Faith, Simply Southern
- **Evidence:** "This sweatshirt is perfect! Simple, clean design, well made and exactly as described. I love it! [from matching family buyer at SBG Co.]"
- **Our advantage:** SBG Co. is the only brand in this competitive set that intentionally designs women's and kids/baby pieces to merchandise together as mom-and-mini coordinates. Kerusso and Elevated Faith cover multiple demographics but don't build collections around the coordinating occasion. This is a distinct, repeatable purchase driver that competitors don't structurally replicate.
- **Ad angle:** She believes it. Now she can wear it — and so can her mini. SBG Co. designs faith pieces for mamas and their littles that actually go together, because matching what's in your heart starts at home.

### Faith apparel that doesn't feel churchy or dated — competitors skew either too preachy or too generic
- **Competitors failing:** Kerusso, Simply Southern
- **Evidence:** "My husband loves wearing Christian t-shirts, but those with deeper spiritual messages are hard to find. Kerusso is unique among the t-shirt companies out there. [Kerusso buyer noting how hard the category is to get right] / Simply Southern is more than just a clothing brand; it's a lifestyle. [Simply Southern skews Southern-lifestyle but lacks faith depth]"
- **Our advantage:** SBG Co. occupies the specific white space between 'Christian bookstore tee' and 'generic lifestyle brand' — faith without preachiness, mom humor without vulgarity, America pride without aggression. The tone itself is the moat. Designs like 'Chasing Babies + Jesus' and 'Loves Jesus + America, Too' fuse faith with real life in a way neither Kerusso's message-heavy approach nor Simply Southern's secular Southern aesthetic achieves.
- **Ad angle:** Faith that fits your actual life — not just Sunday. SBG Co. makes pieces for the woman who loves Jesus and also needs a Diet Coke, because both things are true and she doesn't have to choose which one goes on her shirt.

### Values clarity and brand stance — SpearmintLOVE's social-values vacuum caused a documented mass trust collapse among exactly SBG Co.'s target buyer
- **Competitors failing:** SpearmintLOVE
- **Evidence:** "To all customers who care about black lives matter. I want to inform you that Spearmint is against BLM. With a huge following of 811k, they lost over 100k followers in less than 24hrs due to the incident."
- **Our advantage:** SBG Co.'s faith-forward identity and founder authenticity make its values impossible to misread. A brand named 'Saved by Grace' with 10 years of public Christian positioning does not leave buyers guessing about what it stands for. This is a structural trust advantage over lifestyle brands that try to appeal to everyone and end up standing for nothing — especially among the faith-driven moms who were SpearmintLOVE's core audience and defected en masse.
- **Ad angle:** You know exactly what we stand for — and you always have. SBG Co. has been wearing its faith on its sleeve for 10 years, because a brand that believes something should be able to say so.

### Consistent, true-to-description product representation — competitors' items regularly arrive looking nothing like website photos
- **Competitors failing:** SpearmintLOVE, Elevated Faith
- **Evidence:** "I would not advise spending your money on this sight, items are not good quality and do not look like pictures. Also over priced and unable to return for full refund. [SpearmintLOVE] / Some noted inconsistencies in sizing or product appearance, such as a stain on a t-shirt or a color not matching expectations. [Elevated Faith]"
- **Our advantage:** SBG Co.'s made-to-order production model means each item is created fresh rather than pulled from pre-made stock that may have been misrepresented in photography. On-site reviews cite 'exactly as described' and 'quality is precise' — suggesting photo-to-product fidelity is a genuine strength, not just a claim.
- **Ad angle:** What you see is exactly what arrives. When every piece is made just for you, there's no sitting-in-a-warehouse version to disappoint you — SBG Co. made-to-order means the photo is the promise.

### Sizing confidence for kids and baby — SpearmintLOVE's chronic small-running, mislabeled sizing destroys purchase confidence for gift-buyers and milestone shoppers
- **Competitors failing:** SpearmintLOVE
- **Evidence:** "sizing runs small on baby zipper pjs bought the size she wears in all other brands will only be able to wear for a photo due to being too tight / This company sent clothes that were for a newborn or preemie. And they were labeled size 0 to 3 months. Absolutely not the right size clothing."
- **Our advantage:** SBG Co.'s wholesale platform reviews — from boutique buyers who are highly attuned to sizing accuracy because their retail customers will return items — rate the brand at 4.7 stars with specific callouts for quality and fulfillment accuracy. Boutique buyers are the most demanding sizing validators in apparel retail; strong scores in that channel signal that sizing is reliable and true-to-label.
- **Ad angle:** Buy the size you need — not the size you have to guess. SBG Co. kids and baby pieces are sized reliably enough that 4,000+ boutiques trust us to stock their shelves without surprises.

### Faith community depth beyond apparel — Elevated Faith offers bible study, worship music, and prayer features but with product quality inconsistency; neither delivers both simultaneously
- **Competitors failing:** Elevated Faith, Kerusso
- **Evidence:** "Great Christian community to grow within. They provide resources for bible study, cute apparel and accessories. Awesome music for praise and worship and even working out. [Elevated Faith buyer loving the community layer] / I am so glad to have come across your app! Not only can I order apparel and items to share my faith but my faith can be renewed through the daily devotions available in the app. [Kerusso buyer]"
- **Our advantage:** SBG Co.'s founder-authenticity and 10-year community loyalty create the emotional infrastructure for a deeper faith community play — particularly with its core audience of Christian moms who already feel personal connection to the brand story. Unlike Elevated Faith (which has the community features but broken fulfillment trust) or Kerusso (which has the devotional layer but failing fabric quality), SBG Co. can layer community depth onto a product base that customers already trust and love.
- **Ad angle:** More than a shirt — a reminder of who you are. SBG Co. was built by a woman who believes what it says on every tag, and that's why the pieces land differently than anything you'd find in a Christian bookstore.

## Shared Dealbreakers (Category Vulnerabilities)

- **Quality control inconsistency — apparel that fades, misrepresents color, or arrives defective with no reliable remedy** — affects: Kerusso, Elevated Faith, SpearmintLOVE
  - Our response: SBG Co.'s made-to-order, in-house production model is the structural answer to mass-production QC failures. Lead with 'made one at a time' as both a quality guarantee and an anti-fast-fashion story — it directly counters the category's most painful recurring complaint.

- **Sizing runs small or inconsistent — buyers can't trust their usual size, especially for kids/baby gifts** — affects: SpearmintLOVE, Elevated Faith
  - Our response: SBG Co.'s 4.7-star Faire wholesale rating from boutique buyers — who are the most demanding sizing validators in apparel retail — functions as a credible third-party sizing endorsement. Surface this in creative aimed at gift buyers and first-time purchasers: 'trusted by 4,000+ boutiques means the size you order is the size you get.'

- **Brand values ambiguity — lifestyle and faith-adjacent brands that won't take a clear public stance lose trust with values-driven buyers at the moment of crisis** — affects: SpearmintLOVE, Simply Southern
  - Our response: SBG Co.'s name and 10-year public identity make values ambiguity structurally impossible. This is a passive but powerful advantage — lean into it in brand storytelling by making the clarity of conviction itself a differentiator: 'You've always known what we stand for.'

- **Unresponsive post-purchase experience when products arrive damaged or wrong — customers feel abandoned by faith-branded companies that preach care but don't practice it** — affects: Elevated Faith, Kerusso
  - Our response: While this is an operational issue that cannot be the lead in paid creative, SBG Co. can build implicit trust through its made-to-order model framing — 'made for you, specifically' signals individual accountability that mass-production brands can't credibly claim. The product story itself pre-empts the 'lost in the machine' feeling competitors' buyers describe.

## Defensive Priorities (Objections That Hit Us Too)

- **Long wait time — 14 business days is a real friction point, especially for gift buyers with a specific occasion in mind** — pre-empt: Reframe the wait as proof of craft, not operational lag — 'Made for you, not pulled from a shelf' turns the turnaround from a weakness into a feature. Pair with urgency guidance ('order by [date] for [occasion]') to make the timeline feel plannable rather than punishing. Surface the made-to-order story early in the funnel so the buyer has already emotionally accepted the tradeoff before she hits checkout.

- **Store-credit-only returns with narrow eligibility windows — buyers fear being stuck with a product they can't return for cash** — pre-empt: Every competitor in this space has return friction — SBG Co. is not uniquely punitive here. The differentiating move in creative is to lean so hard on product quality proof ('made one at a time,' wholesale validation, on-site reviews citing 'exactly as described') that the return question becomes less psychologically salient. When buyers trust that the product will arrive right, return policy becomes a secondary concern.

- **Brand name transition from 'Saved by Grace Co.' to 'SBG Co.' creates recognition confusion for existing loyalists and search-level ambiguity for new buyers** — pre-empt: Use 'SBG Co. (formerly Saved by Grace Co.)' consistently across all paid creative, email headers, and packaging until the new handle achieves independent recognition. The parenthetical bridges the loyalty gap with existing buyers and signals continuity of values to new ones. Make the name-evolution part of the brand growth story rather than leaving it as an unexplained discontinuity.

- **Made-to-order model limits ability to offer fast gifting for time-sensitive occasions — a real disadvantage against Kerusso and Elevated Faith's fast-ship positioning** — pre-empt: Even fast-ship competitors fail at gifting occasions — SpearmintLOVE's Christmas delivery collapse and Elevated Faith's wrong-item-sent nightmares show that speed without accuracy is no protection. SBG Co.'s counter-position is 'the right thing, when we say it will arrive' — lean into reliability and accuracy over raw speed, and build a gifting purchase guide with clear order-by date windows to convert occasion-driven buyers before the urgency window closes.

## Category Table Stakes

- **Faith-forward graphic apparel with spiritually meaningful messaging** — Every successful competitor in this space leads with faith identity as the primary purchase trigger. Buyers self-select on values alignment before evaluating design or price.

- **Coverage across women's, kids', and baby categories** — Kerusso, Elevated Faith, and SpearmintLOVE all serve multi-generational households. A faith lifestyle brand that covers only one demographic misses the gifting and family-unit purchase occasions that drive basket size.

- **Designs that function as conversation starters in everyday life** — Customers across Kerusso and Elevated Faith explicitly cite 'starting conversations about faith' as a primary motivation. Apparel that doesn't carry a legible, shareable message underperforms in this category.

- **Perceived product quality that justifies premium pricing** — Buyers in this space are paying $28–50+ for apparel. Softness, durability, and construction quality are threshold expectations — failures here (see Kerusso fading, SpearmintLOVE mislabeling) become hard dealbreakers.

- **Brand mission alignment with charitable giving or community purpose** — Elevated Faith's #1 stated purchase driver is its Kingdom-giving mission. Simply Southern cites animal welfare partnerships. Faith buyers expect the brand to stand for something beyond product.

## Per-Brand Analyses

_Saved by Grace Co. commands genuine emotional loyalty from its core audience — faith-driven moms who love the made-to-order craftsmanship, meaningful designs, and brand authenticity built around founder Whitney Vaughn's story. On Faire's wholesale platform (504 reviews, 4.7 rating), retailer satisfaction is strong, signaling that the product itself is a proven commercial asset. However, the brand's most significant friction points center on its operations: a 14-business-day turnaround, a rigid store-credit-only return policy with a narrow eligibility window, and unresponsive customer service are the primary reasons customers defect and file chargebacks — creating a clear switching opportunity for any competitor that can match SBG Co.'s product heart while offering more buyer-friendly post-purchase policies. Note: several source URLs in the research set belong to unrelated companies (Saved By The Dress, Saving Grace CBD, savinggrace.help), and those signals were excluded or flagged accordingly; findings above are drawn only from sources clearly attributable to savedbygraceco.com and corroborating editorial coverage of the SBG Co. brand._
